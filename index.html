Progress & Resistance Penalty


Each act completed applies −10% to Fire, Cold and Lightning resistance, reaching −60% by endgame. Chaos resistance is unaffected. Stage-to-penalty mapping is approximate for the late steps — the banner shown in game when you enter a new area, or your character sheet, is the authority.
